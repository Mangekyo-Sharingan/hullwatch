# Hullwatch v0.10.0 — mining & fabrication test build

Playable Windows test build with the survival loop: mine the drifting field, haul it
home, and fabricate what the ship eats.

## What's new since v0.9.0
- **Material hold.** Ice, ore, metal and exotic units with a mass budget; the status
  panel (E) gains a CARGO section.
- **Mining runs.** The hull cutter is suit gear now — grab an empty sample canister,
  work an asteroid or debris cluster out on EVA, then stow the load at the cargo port
  on the EVA platform. Deposits are finite and seeded: what you mine stays mined.
- **Anomalies.** Anomalies are read, not cut. Carry the field scanner, study one, and
  the survey is logged while exotic material goes straight into the hold.
- **Fabricator.** A power-gated workshop in the engine bay. Recipes: ice → water
  filter / O2 candle, ore → CO2 filter, metal → gaskets. One job at a time; the output
  lands in stores; refusals (no power, no materials, already running) say why.
- **The loop closes.** A month-long headless scenario proves the ship can sustain
  itself on mined inputs alone: the fabricator replaces the parts the ship wears out.

- **Manual.** A FIELD WORK page documents mining, cargo, anomalies and the fabricator.

Note: start a fresh save — older saves may not know the new devices.
