# October 13 power tuning

## Change

Automatic restoration now requires generator headroom for the restored load and
the existing 25 W margin. A tiny amount of recharged battery energy no longer
restarts an unsustainable load. Batteries still support initially connected
loads, including all fourteen lights, and priority-zero circuits remain connected.
Opening a shed circuit's breaker clears its shed flag without inventing a
restoration event. The manual now describes priority shedding accurately.

SHIP CONTROL / LIGHTING shows total requested watts, actual generation and
stored Wh/capacity beside the switches. The player can see the consequence of a
selection without moving to another program. Fixture ratings remain 25 W indoors
and 10 W exterior; rendering, RTG output, battery capacity and savev7 are unchanged.
