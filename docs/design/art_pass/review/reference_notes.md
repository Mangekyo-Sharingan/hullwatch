# Reference observations for the hero cabin target

**Scope:** visual notes for the user's supplied stills and the official Starpath store/trailer reference. The pictures carry no capture-time metadata, so no trailer timestamps are assigned. Starpath remains an external visual reference; all proposed assets are original Hullwatch work.

## Observable details

| Image | Direct observation | Hullwatch interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| `Reference/ref1.png` | View down a faceted, round-section interior. Several thick transverse frames recede toward a smaller opening. Dark bays sit between lit frame edges; a small work light creates a concentrated warm pool against cool shadow. | Give the cabin a repeated pressure-frame rhythm and leave real negative space between frames. Distinguish structure, mounting recess and loose equipment as separate depth layers. Preserve the center route. |
| `Reference/cockpit.png` | A compact pilot station is ringed by physical CRT faces and raised console pieces. The surrounding pressure frame is visible around the equipment. Warm highlights pick out edges; large portions remain dark. | Keep the live screens clustered around the pilot position and support them with visible rails/brackets. Let the hull frame remain legible behind them. Keep screen faces readable under the analogue filter. |
| `Reference/ref2.png` | Close view of a beige retro computer with an inset dark display, chunky keyboard and thick enclosure. | Favour a few large physical controls and a deep display surround over thin flat UI panels. Use this cue for passive physical housings only; preserve the game's existing interaction map. |
| `review/shot_hero_cockpit.png` | Current cockpit wide view shows an open central aisle and side screens. Rib/console depth is weak in the low, broad lighting; the screenshot includes orientation text and a traffic banner. | Use as a layout baseline. Hide HUD for composition review, then repeat with HUD for usability. Add visible frame depth and improve practical-light shape before adding small decorative detail. |
| `review/shot_hero_utility.png` | Two utility units sit on a mostly open wall under a broad upper beam; each has a simple framed panel. An orientation prompt and traffic banner cover part of the view. | Keep the fittings' present wall positions and build them as backplate, inset case, cover and restrained service routing. Do not fill the intervening wall with arbitrary boxes. |
| `review/shot_hero_quarters.png` | Bunk, locker and hatch approach are present, but surrounding wall/ceiling surfaces read as broad unbroken planes. The same HUD/traffic overlays are visible. | Add the accepted shallow structural framing and a coherent light/material treatment while maintaining the existing bunk, locker, dock, and hatch route. |

## Limits and source

- `ref1.png`, `ref2.png`, and `cockpit.png` are user-provided image files. Their visible browser/player controls and absence of capture metadata mean they are still references, not frame-accurate storyboard records.
- The public [Starpath Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1779540/Starpath/) describes its retro aesthetic and purpose-built screens. No timestamp is attributed to a particular design detail in this note.
- Do not use these screenshots or trailer frames as game textures, meshes, logos, or store art. The useful cues are framing depth, hardware density, physical displays, and light/dark composition.
- The earlier captures show startup/interaction overlays and should not be used to judge final framing or functional legibility. Steps 09 and 12 will capture the accepted final build from the fixed views in `hero_target.md`.
