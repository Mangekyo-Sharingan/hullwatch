# Slice 03 — Investigation and surveying

Date: 2026-10-09. Status: planned. Operator approved the six activity directions and requested implementation to begin with slice 01; detailed values below are design choices to refine, not measured balance.

## Player sequence

Read uncertain contact data, survey at stand-off, select an opportunity, prepare a sample mission and recover information or materials.

## Implementation steps

1. Define useful contact evidence: confidence, composition, recoverable equipment, hazards and expected remaining stock.
2. Persist a survey notebook through the shared computer; records describe readings, not secret Xenonite internals.
3. Differentiate fields/destinations by opportunity distribution and cost; keep travel freely chosen.
4. Add one bounded probe only after instrument-led decisions work: launch, operating cost, sample capacity, retrieval and failure outcome.
5. Preserve discovery and depletion through drift, streaming and revisits; no reward reset from travel.

## Connections

Survey choices direct salvage and gathering; specialist sensors improve information; resources and exterior probe hardware support missions; logs preserve knowledge.

## Acceptance

- Readings change a travel/preparation decision.
- One complete sample/probe loop produces a practical result and charges real costs.
- Unknown sites remain investigable without a campaign unlock; knowledge and finite yields survive reload.
