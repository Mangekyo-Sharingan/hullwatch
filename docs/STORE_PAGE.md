## Title
Hullwatch

## Short description (≤ 300 chars)
A first-person ship-maintenance sim aboard a decaying long-haul freighter. Keep the
power, air, heat and water alive across a multi-decade voyage. There are no monsters
out here. There is only entropy, and the sound the ship makes when something is wrong.

## Long description
You are the operator of the *Hullwatch*-class hauler, awake on schedule while the rest
of the rotation is not. The ship's log insists everything is nominal. The systems
disagree.

Hullwatch is a maintenance simulation about knowing your ship by ear. Every fan, pump
and relay has a healthy sound and a dying one. Read the panels, learn the procedures
from the operator's manual, and keep six compartments breathing while the void works
at the seams.

- Diagnose by instrument and by ear — sound carries information, and silence is a warning
- Repair with real procedures: scrubber filter swaps, breaker work, sealant patches,
  reclaimer service, emergency O2 candles
- Balance a single bus: whatever you power, something else goes dark
- Wear is real: filters clog, bearings whine, plating fatigues, and every fix buys time
- No combat. No jump scares. No entity. The horror is arithmetic.

## Feature bullets (store formatting)
- Systemic ship simulation: power, thermal, atmosphere, water, hull and nav all interlock
- Diegetic interface: status panels, a maintenance board, and a binder you actually read
- Failure cascades you can trace, never scripted gotchas
- Procedural audio placeholders carry diagnostic information (final mix TBD)
- Deterministic simulation with versioned saves
